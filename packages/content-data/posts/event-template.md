## The event

Name, tier, date, format and the newest legal set. Add the event to `data/events.yaml` and set `event: <slug>` in this post's frontmatter: it then appears on the event's page and the calendar.

## Expected field

The decks you expect to face, with rough shares if you have a source. Say where the read comes from (recent results, local scene, online ladder) and how confident you are.

## What I'd play

The deck choice, linked to its Deck Dex entry, and the one or two techs this field calls for.

## Matchups to practice

The two matchups that decide your day, and the line to rehearse for each.

## Report

After the event: your record, the matchups you actually played, and what you'd change. Update `updated` in the frontmatter when you add it.
